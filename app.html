Boost Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out output voltages, duty cycles and inductor ripple currents for an ideal step-up stage from the numbers you type: the input over one minus the duty for the output, one minus input over output for the duty, and the input times the duty over the inductor and the switching rate for the ripple. The voltages, duty cycles, inductors and frequencies are yours to measure or read off your own converter and datasheet. It is the ideal-stage relationship only, so it has not seen your converter and does not know the diode drop, the switch losses, the inductor saturation or the control loop, and it gives no verdict that a boost circuit is adequate or safe. Every number here is your own entry.

The step-up truth

What a duty cycle does: the input over one minus the duty.

The duty truth

The duty cycle a step-up asks for: one minus input over output.

The ripple truth

The current swing: the input times the duty over the inductor and the switching rate.
